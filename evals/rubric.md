# Rubric for the CV tailoring judge

The judge reads the original CV, the job description, and the rewritten CV.
Score each criterion from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent), then give one overall score.

1. **Relevance to the role.** Does the rewrite put the skills the job asks for first?
2. **Factual accuracy.** Does every fact in the rewrite appear in the original CV?
   Any added employer, degree, year, or skill lowers this score sharply.
3. **Completeness.** Does the rewrite keep the important experience from the original?
4. **Format.** Are section headings and bullet points clear, and is the text in the same
   language as the original CV?

The judge must end its reply with one line in this exact form:

SCORE: <number from 1 to 5>

The overall score is the lowest of the four criteria, so one invented fact cannot be
hidden by good scores elsewhere.
